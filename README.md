## Overview
**3 Body Problem** The series portrays a fictional past, present, and future wherein Earth encounters an alien civilization from a nearby system of three Sun-like stars orbiting one another, a representative example of the three-body problem in orbital mechanics.
## Why I like this series?
> I liked this series because i mostly like science fiction mixed with mystery and also loved the idea that the protagonists of the series were introduced to the aliens and the conflict through a VR "game" which piqued my interest since I also like to play games and it easily became one of my favorite moments from the series itself
